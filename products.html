1. Main Features

Photobooth is an interactive photo-taking service that allows customers to take photos quickly and creatively.

W: 1400mm × D: 1200mm × H: 2200mm
CLASSIC FRAME BOOTH
Optical Rig Studio camera & halo lighting
Kiosk Control Full touch-screen workflow
01

High-quality camera and lighting for clear and attractive photos.

Equipped with high-definition optical lenses and balanced illumination to ensure optimal brightness, depth, and clarity for every shot.

02

Touch-screen operation, allowing customers to control the photo-taking process easily.

Intuitive graphical interface enabling users to select layouts, start countdowns, trigger flash, and review shots effortlessly.

03

Various backgrounds, filters, frames, and photo templates to personalize each photo.

Extensive aesthetic catalog featuring Korean trending filters, themed graphic overlays, and customizable border templates.

04

Instant photo printing so customers can receive physical photos immediately.

Commercial thermal dye-sublimation hardware outputting crisp, dry, water-resistant archival photo strips in seconds.

05

Digital photo sharing through QR codes or other digital platforms.

Instant generation of cloud download links and QR codes for downloading high-resolution digital files and live-action GIFs.

06

Customizable designs with event names, logos, themes, or special messages.

Flexible bespoke frame branding for corporate activations, weddings, birthday celebrations, and brand pop-ups.
